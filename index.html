🌙 Sleep Cycle Calculator

Wake up refreshed by timing 90-minute sleep cycles.

How it works

Sleep happens in cycles of about 90 minutes. Waking up at the end of a complete cycle — rather than in the middle of deep sleep — makes you feel rested. This calculator also adds the average 15 minutes it takes to fall asleep. Aim for 5 to 6 cycles (7.5–9 hours).

What are sleep cycles?

Each cycle alternates light sleep, deep sleep and REM (dream) sleep. Deep sleep dominates the early cycles, REM the later ones. Cutting a cycle short often causes grogginess, even after 8 hours in bed.

Tips to fall asleep faster

  • Keep your bedroom between 60–67°F (15–19°C).
  • Avoid screens 30 minutes before bed.
  • Get natural light within an hour of waking.
  • Keep a consistent wake-up time, even on weekends.

FAQ

Are 90-minute cycles exact for everyone?

No. Individual cycles range from 80 to 120 minutes. Use these times as a starting point and adjust by 10–15 minutes based on how you feel.

Is 4 cycles (6 hours) enough?

Occasionally, yes. Long-term, most adults need 5–6 cycles. Chronic short sleep affects memory, mood and immunity.

What if I wake up during the night?

That's normal — brief awakenings often happen at cycle boundaries. If you're awake more than a few minutes, the issue is usually stress, light or noise.

Does this work for naps?

For naps, prefer 20 minutes (light sleep only) or one full 90-minute cycle. In between, you'll likely wake groggy.
